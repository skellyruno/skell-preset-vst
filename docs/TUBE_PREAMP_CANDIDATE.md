# Tube preamp candidate — research only

Independent asymmetric normalized tanh core, first-order ADAA, per-channel state, gain, high-frequency air and DC rejection. Not an Avalon circuit emulation; no third-party code. No live rack routing or parameter changes. The local strict C++17 UBSan test at 48 kHz, 9 kHz sine, drive 5, bias .12, input amplitude .7, measured folded 21 kHz component relative to fundamental: direct -11.950 dBc, ADAA -17.897 dBc, improvement 5.946 dB. This is one core-only alias test, not proof of broadband quality or full preamp improvement. Neutral exact bypass, independent stereo, finite output and reset/nonfinite handling passed at 44.1/48/96/192 kHz. First-order ADAA introduces magnitude/phase changes; measure full-band response, transient and real vocal A/B, CPU, automation smoothing, and compare actual oversampling candidates before enabling live. Do not replace the approved saturator or existing mic modeling.
